## Short description (200 characters max on Indus)

Track your meals, habits, study time and daily route in one private app.

## Long description

Written in plain words on purpose: Indus machine-translates it into 12 Indian languages, and idioms ("streaks", "slips", "nudge") came out wrong. After editing, re-run **Translate Now** for each language in step 3.

Life Tracker is a simple and private app to track your day.

Food: Write down what you ate for breakfast, lunch, snacks and dinner. Pick any day of the week to see what you ate on that day.

Habits: Build good habits. Mark each day you complete a habit and see how many days in a row you have done it. To quit a bad habit, record each time it happens. Life Tracker counts the days since the last time and shows if this week is better than last week.

Focus: A study timer that keeps running even if you close the app. Add a topic, reach your daily study goal and see your last seven days.

Route: See where you went each day on a map. Save your location with one tap, or turn on background tracking to record your route automatically.

Plan: A short setup asks what you want to improve, how much time you have each day, and when to send you a reminder. You can change it at any time.

Private:
• Only you can see your entries.
• No ads and no analytics.
• Route history is deleted automatically after 30 days.
• You can delete your account and all your data from inside the app at any time.

Light and dark themes. Also works in a web browser.

## Notes for the reviewer

- Test sign-in: create an account in the app (Create an account → enter the 6-digit code from the email).
- Background location is optional and off by default. It is switched on only from the Route screen, after an in-app explanation, and is used only to draw the user's own daily route. A foreground-service notification is shown while it runs.
- Account deletion: You (avatar, top right) → Delete account.
